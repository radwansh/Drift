Payroll dashboard questions, answered

What is a payroll dashboard?

A payroll dashboard shows your payroll's key numbers on one screen: total salary cost, headcount, employer cost and what changed since the last period. Clarivo builds yours from the payroll file you already export, so you never build one in Excel again.

How is Clarivo different from tracking payroll in Excel?

In Excel, someone rebuilds the comparison every period, and the checks depend on who builds it. Clarivo compares every employee and every pay component automatically, keeps your payroll history in one place and flags what looks wrong, in one click.

Which payroll systems does Clarivo work with?

Any system that exports to Excel or CSV: SAP, ADP, Sage, local payroll software or your own spreadsheets. Direct connections to SAP, ADP and Sage are on our roadmap.

What payroll anomalies does Clarivo detect?

Net pay that changed by 20% or more with no change in any pay component, pay components that drop to zero, new or missing components, joiners and leavers, and data issues such as a missing pay period. Each alert says who, what and why.

Do I need IT help to set it up?

No. Upload a file and Clarivo maps your columns the first time; confirm once and it remembers. There is nothing to install and nothing to connect.

Does Clarivo replace my payroll software?

No. Your payroll system still calculates and pays salaries. Clarivo checks what it produced and shows you what changed before you approve. It never changes your data.

What happens to my payroll data?

Each company's data is kept separate and encrypted, and it is never used to train AI models. The AI only recognises your columns, writes a plain-language summary and ranks alerts; it never changes a number.

Which pay frequencies and currencies does it handle?

Monthly, bi-monthly, bi-weekly and weekly payrolls, in any currency. Clarivo never adds amounts in different currencies together; each currency is reported on its own.

How much does Clarivo cost?

Clarivo is free during the beta, with no credit card. When billing starts, plans run from €59 to €299 a month by company size, and beta founders pay half: €29 to €149 a month for 24 months.
